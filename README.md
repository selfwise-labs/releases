# Selfwise Labs apps

Three personal apps by Cristian Toader. Each one runs on Android and iPhone, keeps its data on
the phone, and has an optional account that syncs between your phones.

| App | What it is | iPhone | Android |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Selfwise** | A personal organizer: journal, plan, sleep, activities with levels. | [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/hzXY9tcd) | [Download APK](https://github.com/selfwise-labs/releases/releases/download/selfwise-latest/selfwise.apk) |
| **Sagax** | A strength and endurance training log with periodised plans. | [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/EQMKsmwa) | [Download APK](https://github.com/selfwise-labs/releases/releases/download/sagax-latest/sagax.apk) |
| **Sapor** | A food diary built around photos, with full nutrition data. | [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/mnCAbn61) | [Download APK](https://github.com/selfwise-labs/releases/releases/download/sapor-latest/sapor.apk) |

## Installing

**iPhone**: install the TestFlight app from the App Store, then open the app's TestFlight link
above and tap Install. TestFlight keeps the app updated.

**Android**: open the Download APK link on the phone. When the download finishes, open the file
and allow the browser to install apps if Android asks. Updating later works the same way: the
new APK installs over the old one and keeps your data.

## Updates

Most updates arrive on their own: open the app, and the next start runs the new version. When an
update needs a reinstall, the app shows an Update button that brings you here.

All releases, with version numbers: [Releases](https://github.com/selfwise-labs/releases/releases).
