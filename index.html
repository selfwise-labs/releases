The apps

One for each part of the day

Real screens, demo data. Each app is free, works offline and has no feed, no ads and no subscription.

Selfwise

Journal, plan, sleep

A personal organizer. The day is a timeline: sleep, a morning routine, work, the things you planned and the things you did. Activities level up the more you do them, and the analytics show where the time went.

  • Morning, work and evening sections with checklists
  • Activities with levels, XP and streak multipliers
  • Sleep against your plan, reminders before planned items

Sagax

Strength and endurance

A training log. Record every set, run, ride and swim; follow a plan that sets the next weights from what you lifted; and watch the trends: volume, estimated 1RM per lift, running fitness, load per muscle.

  • Plans (5/3/1, mesocycles, double progression, 5 km, 10 km, FTP builder)
  • Runs from Health Connect or logged by hand, with zones and est. VO₂max
  • Programs written by your assistant, straight into the app
iPhone · TestFlight Android · APK

Sapor

Food diary, from photos

Snap the plate and your assistant names the foods and fills in the full nutrient profile, down to the micronutrients, against your targets. Or search the USDA database and log by the gram.

  • Meals, saved meals and targets for calories and macros
  • Every mineral and vitamin of the day against the reference intake
  • Daily weigh-in with a 7-day average

Claude & ChatGPT

Talk to your data

One connector gives your assistant the data of all three apps: ask about your training, your week or what you ate, have it write a training program, or label a meal photo. Two steps in the app, then one in the assistant you use.

1

Create an account in any of the apps: Account, Create account, with your invite code. The same account works in all three.

2

Create a token on the Account screen, name it after the assistant and copy it. You can revoke it there any time.

3

In Claude

On claude.ai: Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector. Paste the URL, choose No sign-in, and add one request header. Connectors added on the web also work in the Claude phone app.

https://mcp.selfwiselabs.com/mcp
Authorization: Bearer <your token>
Claude Code (terminal) →
claude mcp add --transport http --scope user selfwise https://mcp.selfwiselabs.com/mcp --header "Authorization: Bearer <your token>"
3

In ChatGPT

ChatGPT cannot send a header, so the token goes in the URL. On chatgpt.com/plugins (paid plans): the plus button, Add custom MCP server, paste the URL, choose No authentication, then Create as a plugin and Install it.

https://mcp.selfwiselabs.com/mcp/<your token>

Anyone with that URL can read your data: keep it to yourself, and revoke the token from the Account screen if it leaks.

Codex (terminal) →

Codex can send the header. Put the token in the environment variable SELFWISE_TOKEN and add to ~/.codex/config.toml:

[mcp_servers.selfwise] url = "https://mcp.selfwiselabs.com/mcp" bearer_token_env_var = "SELFWISE_TOKEN"
